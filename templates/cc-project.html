Comparative concepts project

Our project explores how the comparative concepts of Croft (CCs; 2022) help with construction annotation. We utilise the CCs as a tool within the MoCCA framework (Model of Comparative Concepts for Aligning Constructicons; Lorenzi et al. 2024) which aims at comparing constructions and connecting constructicons cross-linguistically.

On this page you can navigate through some already annotated constructions (of the CASA constructicon of English and in the future the FrameNet Constructicon of German and the Russian constructicon) to illustrate their comparability and connectivity.

The CC-project is still under development.

CC-project webpage is hosted by the Research Constructicon, a web application that serves as database connecting the contributions of the Research Training Group Dimensions of Constructional Space at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg.
